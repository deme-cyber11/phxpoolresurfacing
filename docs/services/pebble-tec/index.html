Pebble Tec Pool Resurfacing in Phoenix — 15–20 Years of Durability

Pebble Tec is the upgrade for Phoenix homeowners who are tired of the replastering cycle. The aggregate finish is stain-resistant, naturally slip-resistant, and built specifically to handle intense UV, alkaline water, and the thermal swings of Arizona summers. It's the longest-lasting residential pool finish available — and it looks exceptional for every year of its life.

When You're Done with the Replastering Cycle

If you've replastered once already and you're staring down round two, you already know the math doesn't feel right. Plaster in Phoenix holds 7–10 years when maintained perfectly. Pebble Tec holds 15–20. The upfront difference pays for itself well before the plaster would need replacing again.

Or maybe it's not about the math — it's about the pool that stains no matter what you do, the algae that keeps finding grip in the rough surface, the chemistry costs that climb every season. Pebble Tec's non-porous aggregate surface doesn't absorb chemicals the way plaster does. Algae has nowhere to grip. Calcium runs off instead of building up. Your maintenance load drops — often significantly — in the first season after installation.

Most homeowners who switch from plaster to Pebble Tec say the same thing: they wish they'd done it sooner.

Questions We Hear Most

What's the difference between Pebble Tec, PebbleSheen, and PebbleFina?

Pebble Tec uses the original larger aggregate (more texture, very durable). PebbleSheen uses a smaller aggregate for a smoother feel underfoot. PebbleFina is the finest texture — nearly as smooth as quartz but with the pebble durability. We'll help you choose based on your preferences and budget.

How long does Pebble Tec last in Arizona?

15–20 years with proper chemistry maintenance. Pebble Tec is specifically engineered to withstand high UV, alkaline water, and thermal cycling — the exact conditions Phoenix pools face year-round.

Can I swim right after installation?

You'll be swimming within 7–10 days of installation. The full 30-day break-in protocol involves specific chemistry and brushing — we walk you through it. After 30 days, full unrestricted use.

Does Pebble Tec work with saltwater systems?

Yes — and it's actually better suited to salt systems than plaster. The denser aggregate is more resistant to the mild acidity salt electrolysis can create over time.
