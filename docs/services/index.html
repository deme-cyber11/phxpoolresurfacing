What We Do

Our Pool Resurfacing Services

We specialize in one thing: restoring and upgrading pool surfaces. Every service below is done by our own trained crew with Arizona-specific materials.

Plaster Resurfacing

Classic white and colored plaster finishes that are smooth, durable, and cost-effective. The most common resurface in Phoenix — we've perfected it for Arizona water chemistry.

Learn More →

Pebble Tec & Aggregate

Textured aggregate finishes that last 15–20 years. Dramatically more durable than plaster with a natural stone look that holds color beautifully under the Phoenix sun.

Learn More →

Quartz Finishes

Quartz finishes bridge the gap between plaster and Pebble Tec — smooth to the touch, stain-resistant, and available in a wide range of colors for a refined look.

Learn More →

Tile & Coping Repair

Cracked coping, loose waterline tile, and grout erosion are all fixable without a full resurface. We repair and replace to match — precise color and material matching included.

Learn More →

Pool Deck Resurfacing

Faded, cracked, or slippery decks are a safety hazard and an eyesore. We resurface with cool-deck coatings, Kool Deck, and stamped concrete — cooler underfoot in the Arizona heat.

Learn More →

Draining & Acid Wash

When algae, calcium buildup, or staining won't respond to chemicals, an acid wash restores the plaster surface. We drain, clean, treat, and refill — all safely within Phoenix water restrictions.

Learn More →
