What Is Acid Wash?

An acid wash, sometimes referred to as an "acid start" or "drain and clean," is a specialized pool cleaning technique that involves draining the pool completely and then applying a diluted muriatic acid solution to the exposed interior surface. This powerful solution etches away a thin layer of plaster, effectively removing stubborn stains, calcium deposits, mineral buildup, and algae that regular brushing and chemicals cannot touch. The process requires precise application and immediate neutralization to prevent damage to the pool’s surface. While primarily used for plaster and pebble finishes, it’s a highly effective method for rejuvenating severely stained pool interiors without the need for full resurfacing. PHX Pool Resurfacing employs trained professionals who understand the delicate balance required to safely and effectively perform an acid wash, ensuring a clean and undamaged finish.

Benefits of Professional Acid Wash

  • Restores Original Luster: An acid wash meticulously strips away years of accumulated mineral stains, hard water lines, and unsightly algae growth, revealing the fresh, vibrant surface underneath. This dramatically enhances your pool's visual appeal, making it look much newer and cleaner.
  • Removes Stubborn Stains and Deposits: Unlike standard cleaning, acid washing is uniquely effective at dissolving tenacious calcium deposits, rust stains, and deep-seated organic discoloration that have become embedded in the plaster. It’s the ultimate solution for blemishes that have resisted all other cleaning efforts.
  • Extends Surface Lifespan: By removing corrosive mineral buildup and preventing deep-set algae from deteriorating the plaster, acid washing helps preserve the structural integrity and longevity of your pool's interior finish. Regular professional acid washes can postpone the need for a full resurfacing.
  • Improves Water Quality and Balance: A thoroughly cleaned pool surface eliminates hiding spots for algae and bacteria, making it easier to maintain balanced water chemistry and reducing the need for excessive chemicals. This contributes to healthier and more enjoyable swimming conditions for everyone.

Our Acid Wash Process

At PHX Pool Resurfacing, our acid wash process is executed with meticulous care, prioritizing both effectiveness and safety. We understand the delicate nature of applying strong chemicals to your pool's surface, which is why our certified technicians follow a stringent, step-by-step procedure. From the initial inspection to the final refill, every stage is managed professionally to ensure a thorough cleaning without compromising your pool’s integrity or surrounding landscape. Our commitment to excellence means you receive a superior clean that rejuvenates your pool's appearance and sets the stage for optimal water quality. We prepare the site, protect surrounding elements, and safely handle all chemicals, ensuring a seamless and worry-free experience for Phoenix homeowners.

  1. Pool Draining and Preparation: We begin by completely draining your pool, carefully managing water runoff and preparing the surrounding area to protect landscaping. All safety protocols are established before chemical application.
  2. Precise Acid Application: Our trained technicians then apply a carefully diluted muriatic acid solution evenly across the pool's surface, working systematically to ensure consistent coverage. This targets and dissolves embedded stains and calcium deposits.
  3. Rinsing and Neutralization: Immediately after the acid has worked, the pool is thoroughly rinsed with water to wash away loosened debris and neutralized to restore a safe pH level. This prevents acid damage to the plaster or ecological impact.
  4. Final Inspection and Refill: Once neutralized, we conduct a comprehensive inspection to ensure all stains are removed and the surface is immaculate. Your pool is then refilled with fresh water, ready for you to enjoy.

Contact PHX Pool Resurfacing for a free, no-obligation estimate tailored to your specific pool.

How long does Acid Wash take?

The entire acid wash process, including draining, cleaning, neutralizing, and refilling, usually takes between 1 to 3 days. The actual acid application and rinsing are quite fast, often completed within a few hours. However, the draining and refilling stages are the most time-consuming parts of the service.

How do I know if I need Acid Wash?

You likely need an acid wash if your pool surface shows significant discoloration, persistent hard water lines, or stubborn calcium deposits that resist regular brushing and chemical treatments. If your pool plaster looks dull, dingy, or has a brownish/greenish tint even after balancing chemicals, it’s a clear sign that a professional acid wash is needed to restore its cleanliness and aesthetic appeal.
