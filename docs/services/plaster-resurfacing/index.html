What Is Plaster Resurfacing?

Plaster resurfacing involves applying a fresh layer of cementitious material to the interior shell of your swimming pool. This process begins by draining the pool completely and preparing the existing surface, often requiring chipping out loose or delaminated plaster, patching imperfections, and ensuring a clean, sound base for adhesion. Our skilled technicians then meticulously apply a new coat of plaster, typically a mixture of white cement, marble dust, and water, to create a smooth, durable, and watertight finish. The goal is to eliminate rough spots, cover stains, and seal minor cracks, providing a renewed protective barrier for your pool's structure. Once applied, the new plaster is carefully cured to achieve optimal hardness and longevity, transforming your pool's appearance and functionality.

Why Phoenix, AZ Properties Need Plaster Resurfacing

Phoenix, AZ's unique desert environment presents specific challenges that make professional plaster resurfacing an essential service for local pool owners. The relentless summer heat and intense UV radiation accelerate the breakdown of pool plaster, causing it to fade, crack, and become rough much faster than in cooler climates. High evaporation rates mean more frequent water refilling, often with hard water, which can lead to mineral deposits and staining on deteriorated plaster. Furthermore, the sandy soil and dust common in Phoenix can be abrasive to rough pool surfaces, contributing to wear and tear. Many homes in Phoenix feature pools built decades ago, and their original plaster is simply past its prime. Given the pool-centric lifestyle here, maintaining a clean, inviting, and structurally sound pool is paramount for comfort, enjoyment, and property value, making timely resurfacing a wise investment against the elements.

Common Questions About Plaster Resurfacing

How much does Plaster Resurfacing cost in Phoenix, AZ?

The cost of plaster resurfacing in Phoenix, AZ, typically ranges from $4,000 to $10,000 or more, depending on the pool's size, depth, existing condition, and the specific type of plaster chosen. Factors like custom features, crack repairs, and the need for extensive surface preparation can also influence the final price. We provide transparent, no-obligation estimates after a thorough on-site evaluation.

How long does Plaster Resurfacing take?

The actual plaster application process for a standard residential pool in Phoenix usually takes 1-3 days. However, including draining, surface preparation, and the initial curing and water-filling period, the entire project typically spans 3-7 days before you can begin the chemical balancing process. Weather conditions can sometimes affect this timeline.

How do I know if I need Plaster Resurfacing?

Clear signs you need plaster resurfacing include a rough, abrasive surface that snags swimsuits or feet, persistent staining or discoloration that won't clean off, noticeable cracks or pitting in the plaster, and areas where the plaster is flaking, peeling, or delaminating. Excessive algae growth that quickly returns even after treatment can also indicate a compromised surface. Leaks in the pool shell might also necessitate resurfacing.
