About Us — Phoenix Pool Resurfacing Specialists

A locally operated team serving the Phoenix metro — we resurface pools, nothing else, and that focus is the whole point

Why We Exist

Most Phoenix homeowners dealing with a failing pool surface have had the same experience: a contractor who does everything from cleaning to construction, sends a crew they've never met, and delivers results that vary wildly by job. We built this business around a different idea — specialization over generalization.

We don't clean pools. We don't build pools. We don't install equipment. We resurface pools — plaster, quartz aggregate, Pebble Tec, tile and coping, decks — and we've done it long enough to know exactly what Phoenix conditions do to pool surfaces and exactly how to counteract them. That focus is not a marketing angle. It's why our results hold up.

What We Do

We specialize in pool resurfacing for Phoenix metro homeowners and businesses. Services include plaster replastering, quartz aggregate finishes, Pebble Tec and premium aggregate installation, waterline tile replacement, coping repair and replacement, pool deck resurfacing, and draining and acid wash. We serve Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria, and surrounding communities across the East and West Valley.

Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. Every quote is written and fixed before work begins. Every job is done by our own crew — not a subcontractor hired for the week.

What Makes Us Different

Three things homeowners tell us matter most after they've worked with us:

Written fixed-price quotes, no exceptions. We visit your pool, assess the actual condition, and quote the full job in writing before any work starts. The number we give you is the number you pay — not a starting range that grows once we're on-site. If we discover something unexpected after work begins, we stop, show you, and explain the options before proceeding.

No subcontractors. Your pool is resurfaced by our trained crew, not someone we hired off a board last week. This is how we maintain consistent quality across every job. The people we send to your property are the same people who do the work.

We call back the same day. If you submit a form or leave a message, you hear from us within a few hours — not days. We know you're comparing options. We make it easy to get the information you need quickly so you can make a decision and move on.

Who We Serve

We serve homeowners and property managers across the greater Phoenix metropolitan area. Our primary service areas include Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, and Peoria. We also regularly work in Surprise, Goodyear, Avondale, Fountain Hills, Paradise Valley, Queen Creek, Sun City, Ahwatukee, and surrounding communities. If you're in the Phoenix metro and your pool needs resurfacing, call us — we can almost certainly help.

Our crews know the Valley's specific water conditions, soil characteristics, and the HOA requirements that apply in many communities. That local knowledge means fewer surprises mid-project.

Our Commitment

Every job gets a written quote before any work starts. If we find something we didn't expect, we stop and show you before any additional work proceeds. The price we quote is the price you pay. The work is done by our crew, not a subcontractor. And if something isn't right after we're done, we come back and fix it — that's what the workmanship warranty is for.

About Mike Torres, Founder

Mike Torres started Phoenix Pool Resurfacing in 2015 after spending several years working as a pool contractor for a general pool company. What he kept noticing: resurfacing was always treated as an afterthought. Equipment techs, chemical guys, construction crews — everyone had a lane. Resurfacing was the job nobody owned.

So he went out on his own and made it the only job.

"General pool companies juggle equipment, chemicals, and construction. We do one thing — resurfacing — so we've gotten exceptionally good at it." That's still the operating principle. The same focus that drove the decision in 2015 drives every hire, every process, every job today.

Mike grew up in the Valley. His family has been in Phoenix since the 80s. He knows what 115-degree summers do to plaster, what hard Valley water does to finishes over time, and why most out-of-state resurfacing estimates are priced for a different climate. That local context isn't a tagline — it's background knowledge that shows up in how jobs get scoped and quoted.
