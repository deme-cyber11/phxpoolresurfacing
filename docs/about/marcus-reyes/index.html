Marcus Reyes

Senior Pool Finishing Consultant · NPC Member · Pebble Tec Certified Applicator

Marcus has spent 18+ years on Arizona pools — plaster, Pebble Tec, quartz aggregate, tile, and coping. He reviews technical content on Phoenix Pool Resurfacing to make sure the guidance homeowners read matches what actually holds up under 110°F summers and calcium-heavy Valley water.

He's a member of the National Plasterers Council (NPC) and a Pebble Tec Certified Applicator. Before moving into consulting work, he ran resurfacing crews across Maricopa County and trained applicators on mix-water ratios, plaster chemistry, and start-up procedures specific to the Sonoran climate.

Areas of expertise

  • Plaster resurfacing & start-up chemistry
  • Pebble Tec & quartz aggregate application
  • Calcium scale management (Phoenix water)
  • Waterline tile & coping stone repair
  • Pool draining & acid wash procedure
  • Maricopa County drainage & disposal rules

Editorial standards

Every cost range, timeline, and product comparison on this site is reviewed against current Phoenix-metro pricing and current manufacturer spec sheets. When a number changes, the page changes. Pages are re-reviewed every 180 days or sooner if a major product release or code change happens.

If you spot something on this site that's wrong or out of date, email contact@phxpoolresurfacing.com and it goes to Marcus for review.
