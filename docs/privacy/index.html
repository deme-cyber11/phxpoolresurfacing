Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Phoenix Pool Resurfacing ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you use phxpoolresurfacing.com or contact us for pool resurfacing services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out our estimate request form, call us, or email us. This typically includes your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about your pool project. We also collect standard web analytics data (pages visited, referrer, approximate location, device and browser type) through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your estimate request, provide a quote, and communicate about scheduling
  • To provide customer service before, during, and after a project
  • To send occasional follow-up communications about your project or service
  • To improve our website and understand which content visitors find useful
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing Your Information

Contact form submissions are transmitted securely to our internal lead management system. We do not share your submission with third-party marketing platforms.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure usage and improve the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work as expected if you do.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the phone number or email below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page indicates when it was last revised.
