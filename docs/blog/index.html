Pool Resurfacing Blog & Guides

Expert guides for Phoenix homeowners — real costs, real timelines, no marketing fluff.

Straight-up answers about pool resurfacing in Phoenix — what it actually costs, how long it takes, which finish lasts longest in our climate, and when to replaster versus upgrade to Pebble Tec. Written by people who actually do the work, for homeowners who want real numbers instead of vague ranges.

Most of the content you'll find about pool resurfacing online is written by marketing teams who've never touched a trowel. That's a problem when you're spending $5,000 to $12,000 on a surface that needs to last a decade in the harshest pool environment in the country. Phoenix sits at the intersection of extreme UV exposure, hard mineral-heavy water, and 115°F summer air temperatures — a combination that punishes pool finishes in ways homeowners in milder climates never have to think about.

These guides are built to answer the questions we actually get asked on estimate calls: What's a fair price for my pool size? Why is my plaster rough after only 8 years? Is Pebble Tec worth the upgrade over quartz? How long will my pool be out of service? We publish the real ranges, the real timelines, and the reasoning behind each recommendation — with no upsell agenda.
