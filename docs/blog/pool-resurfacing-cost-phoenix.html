How Much Does Pool Resurfacing Cost in Phoenix, AZ? [2025]

A complete 2025 pricing breakdown for Phoenix metro pool owners — plaster, Pebble Tec, and quartz finishes with Arizona-specific cost factors.

If your Phoenix pool is showing signs of wear — chalky surface, rough texture, staining, or exposed aggregate — resurfacing is likely in your near future. The question every pool owner asks: how much is this going to cost? Here's an honest, detailed breakdown for 2025.

Pool Resurfacing Cost in Phoenix — By Finish Type

Standard Plaster — $4,000 to $7,000

White marcite plaster is the most affordable resurfacing option. It's a mix of white cement, marble dust, and water applied to the prepared pool shell. In the Phoenix metro, plaster resurfacing for an average 12,000–15,000 gallon pool runs $4,000–$7,000 including draining, surface prep, application, fill, and startup chemistry.

Lifespan in Arizona: 7–12 years. Phoenix's intense UV exposure, extreme heat (pool surface temps exceeding 100°F), and hard water with high calcium content are particularly hard on plaster. The UV and heat accelerate deterioration, and calcium scaling creates rough, stained surfaces faster than in cooler climates.

Pebble Tec / Aggregate Finishes — $8,000 to $15,000

Pebble Tec is the most popular premium pool finish in Arizona — and for good reason. Small natural pebbles are embedded in a cement base, creating a durable, textured surface that resists the desert's punishment far better than plaster. Average cost for a Phoenix metro pool: $8,000–$15,000 depending on pool size and Pebble Tec product line chosen.

Lifespan in Arizona: 15–20+ years. Pebble Tec's natural aggregate composition handles UV, heat, and hard water significantly better than plaster. Many Arizona Pebble Tec installations last 20+ years before needing replacement — making it the most cost-effective option when calculated per year of service.

Quartz Finishes — $6,000 to $12,000

Quartz finishes use quartz aggregate in a cement base, creating a smooth, sparkling surface. They're more durable than plaster but generally not as long-lasting as Pebble Tec in Arizona's extreme conditions. Average Phoenix metro cost: $6,000–$12,000.

Lifespan in Arizona: 12–18 years. A solid middle ground between plaster and Pebble Tec in both cost and durability.

Phoenix-Specific Factors That Affect Cost

  • Pool size: Phoenix metro pools average 12,000–18,000 gallons. Larger pools mean more surface area and higher cost. Pricing is typically calculated per square foot of pool surface.
  • Current surface condition: A pool with significant delamination, structural cracks, or failed previous coatings requires more aggressive surface preparation — bond coat, crack repair, or shell repair before the new finish is applied.
  • Tile and coping: If your waterline tile or coping is damaged, replacing it during resurfacing is significantly cheaper than doing it as a separate project. Budget $1,500–$4,000 for tile/coping work.
  • Drain and refill: Phoenix water costs are relatively low, but filling a 15,000-gallon pool runs $50–$100 in city water. The bigger hidden cost is startup chemistry — proper initial chemical balancing is critical and runs $100–$300.
  • Season: Resurfacing during peak season (October–February) may cost 5–10% more due to contractor demand. Late spring can be a value window before temperatures make work impossible.

Plaster vs. Pebble Tec in Phoenix's Desert Climate

This is the most common question Phoenix pool owners ask. Here's the bottom line:

In a mild climate, plaster can last 10–15 years and the cost difference may not be justified. But in Phoenix — where UV intensity is among the highest in the US, pool water temperatures regularly exceed 90°F, and hard water accelerates mineral deposits — plaster degrades noticeably faster. Most Phoenix plaster pools show significant wear by year 7–8. Pebble Tec handles these conditions with minimal degradation for 15–20+ years.

Over 20 years: two plaster jobs at $5,500 avg = $11,000. One Pebble Tec job at $11,000 avg = $11,000 — same cost but with a consistently better surface throughout. For pool owners planning to stay in their home, Pebble Tec is the clear value winner in Arizona.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pool resurfacing cost in Phoenix in 2025?

Pool resurfacing in Phoenix ranges from $4,000–$15,000+ depending on finish type. Plaster: $4,000–$7,000. Quartz: $6,000–$12,000. Pebble Tec: $8,000–$15,000. All prices include draining, prep, materials, fill, and startup chemistry.

Is Pebble Tec worth the extra cost in Arizona?

For most Arizona pool owners, yes. Pebble Tec lasts 15–20+ years vs. plaster's 7–12 years in Phoenix's extreme UV and hard water conditions. Over a 20-year period, one Pebble Tec job costs about the same as two plaster jobs — with a consistently better surface throughout.

What is the best time of year to resurface a pool in Phoenix?

October through March. Cooler temperatures allow proper curing, and you keep your pool available during the critical summer months. March–April is the last good window before Phoenix temperatures climb above 100°F.
