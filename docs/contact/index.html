What Is Pool Resurfacing and Replastering?

Pool resurfacing, often synonymous with replastering, is the process of stripping away the old, deteriorated interior finish of your swimming pool and applying a brand-new, durable surface. This isn't merely a cosmetic fix; it’s a comprehensive restoration vital for the structural integrity and longevity of your pool. The process typically involves draining the pool, meticulously preparing the underlying concrete shell by chipping out the existing plaster or bond coat, and then applying a fresh layer of finish. PHX Pool Resurfacing utilizes a variety of high-quality materials, including traditional white plaster for a classic look, durable quartz aggregate finishes for added strength and color, and premium pebble finishes for unparalleled beauty and longevity. These materials create a waterproof barrier, protect the pool's shell, and provide a smooth, inviting surface for years of enjoyment, ensuring a superior swimming experience.

Why Phoenix, AZ Properties Need Pool Resurfacing and Replastering

Phoenix, AZ, presents unique challenges for swimming pool longevity, making professional pool resurfacing and replastering not just a luxury, but a necessity for homeowners. The intense desert heat and relentless UV radiation cause plaster to break down much faster than in cooler climates, leading to premature fading, chalking, and cracking. Additionally, Phoenix’s hard water often results in stubborn mineral deposits and scale buildup, further deteriorating pool surfaces and making them unsightly. Given that pools are used extensively year-round in Phoenix, this constant exposure to chemicals and bather load accelerates wear. For older homes common in many Phoenix neighborhoods, original pool plaster may be well past its expected lifespan. Investing in a professional resurfacing protects your pool's structure, prevents costly leaks, and ensures your outdoor investment remains a beautiful and functional escape from the Arizona sun.

Common Questions About Pool Resurfacing and Replastering

How much does Pool Resurfacing and Replastering cost in Phoenix, AZ?

The cost of pool resurfacing in Phoenix, AZ, varies significantly based on pool size, chosen material, and required prep work. Standard white plaster might range from $4,000 to $8,000 for an average-sized pool. More durable and aesthetic finishes like quartz aggregate can cost between $7,000 and $12,000, while premium pebble finishes, known for their longevity and beauty, often range from $10,000 to $18,000 or more. A personalized estimate from PHX Pool Resurfacing provides an accurate figure.

How long does Pool Resurfacing and Replastering take?

The entire pool resurfacing and replastering process typically takes between 1 to 2 weeks from start to finish. The draining, preparation, and application of the new material usually take 3 to 7 days. Following this, the pool must be carefully refilled and the new surface allowed to cure, which involves precise water chemistry balancing and initial brushing over several days. Weather conditions can also slightly impact the timeline.

How do I know if I need Pool Resurfacing and Replastering?

Several clear signs indicate your pool may need resurfacing. These include a rough or abrasive texture on the pool floor and walls that can scrape skin, persistent stains that won't clean, noticeable cracks or pitting in the plaster, chalky residue coming off the walls, or consistent leaks. If your pool water appears cloudy or discolored even after chemical treatment, or if the surface shows significant fading and deterioration, it's time to consider a professional resurface.
