Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing And Replastering in Glendale

How much does pool resurfacing and replastering cost in Glendale?

Costs for pool resurfacing and replastering in Glendale vary based on pool size, chosen finish (from traditional plaster to premium pebble or quartz), and the existing condition of your pool. Glendale's diverse housing market means we see a range from smaller, older pools needing basic refreshes to larger, custom designs requiring specialized materials. Factors include accessibility, necessary repairs, and current material costs specific to the Phoenix metro area. We offer detailed, transparent quotes after an on-site evaluation.

How long does pool resurfacing and replastering take for a Glendale home?

Typically, pool resurfacing and replastering for an average-sized Glendale residential pool takes approximately 5-7 days of active work, excluding the vital drying and curing period. Factors like Glendale's intense weather (which can accelerate curing), pool size, and the specific finish chosen can influence the timeline. Older homes in Glendale, especially those with more extensive pre-existing damage, often require additional preparation time, potentially extending the overall project duration.

How does Glendale's hard water affect my pool plaster, and what can PHX Pool Resurfacing do?

Glendale, like much of Phoenix, has notoriously hard water, rich in calcium and minerals. This can lead to unsightly calcium scaling, etching, and premature degradation of your pool plaster. Our resurfacing solutions, especially quartz and pebble finishes, offer enhanced resistance to these mineral deposits, ensuring a longer-lasting, more beautiful surface for your Glendale pool. We also advise on best practices for water chemistry maintenance to mitigate hard water effects and preserve your new finish.
