Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing in Gilbert

What does pool resurfacing cost in Gilbert, AZ?

Gilbert pool resurfacing costs $3,500-$10,000. Most Gilbert pools fall in the $5,000-$8,000 range for mid-tier finishes. We offer free estimates to give you an exact price based on your pool's specific needs.

Do you serve Power Ranch and Val Vista Lakes in Gilbert?

Yes, we serve all Gilbert neighborhoods including Power Ranch, Val Vista Lakes, Agritopia, Morrison Ranch, Seville, and all surrounding communities. Free on-site consultations available.

How often should Gilbert pools be resurfaced?

In Gilbert's climate, plaster pools need resurfacing every 7-12 years, while Pebble Tec lasts 15-20 years. Hard water and sun exposure are the main factors. Regular maintenance extends surface life.

What pool finish colors work best in Gilbert?

Gilbert homeowners often choose earth tones like French Gray, Blue Granite, or Aqua Blue for Pebble Tec. These complement desert landscaping and create beautiful water colors. We bring samples to your home.

Is Pebble Tec better than plaster for Gilbert pools?

Pebble Tec lasts nearly twice as long as plaster in Gilbert's conditions and is more stain-resistant. It costs more upfront but saves money long-term. Plaster is better if you're on a tight budget or selling soon.
