Frequently Asked Questions — Pool Resurfacing in Ahwatukee

What are the common pool problems in Ahwatukee homes?

Ahwatukee's housing stock is primarily from the 1980s and 1990s, meaning many pools are 25 to 40 years old. Common issues include severely worn plaster with exposed aggregate, calcium buildup that has permanently stained the surface, and plaster delamination. These pools often benefit most from a full replaster with a premium quartz or pebble finish.

How long does a complete pool replaster take in Ahwatukee?

A complete replaster in Ahwatukee typically takes 7 to 10 days for older pools that need more surface preparation. We drain, prep carefully, apply the new surface, and refill — handling the full process from start to finish.
