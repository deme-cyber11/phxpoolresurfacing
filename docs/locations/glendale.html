Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — Our certified team evaluates your pool's current condition, identifies surface damage, structural concerns, and equipment issues, and recommends the optimal resurfacing solution for your Glendale home.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — We drain the pool, strip away failed or deteriorated surface material, complete any necessary structural repairs, and apply a high-quality bond coat to prepare for the new finish.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Your selected finish — plaster, Pebble Tec, PebbleSheen, or quartz aggregate — is hand-applied by our experienced crew with the precision and consistency needed for a beautiful, lasting result.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — The pool is filled on a carefully managed schedule and water chemistry is monitored and adjusted throughout the curing period to protect the new surface from etching, staining, or discoloration.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — We conduct a full quality review, verify water chemistry levels, and walk you through a detailed maintenance plan so your resurfaced pool stays in top condition for years.
