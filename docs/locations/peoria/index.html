Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing in Peoria

What does pool resurfacing cost in Peoria, AZ?

Peoria pool resurfacing ranges from $3,500-$9,500. Most Peoria homeowners spend $5,000-$7,500 for a quality mid-range finish. We provide free on-site estimates with no obligation.

Do you service the Vistancia and Lake Pleasant areas?

Yes, we serve all of Peoria including Vistancia, Lake Pleasant, Sunrise Heights, Westwing Mountain, and all surrounding areas. No pool in Peoria is too far for our team.

What finish options are best for Peoria's climate?

Pebble Tec and quartz finishes perform best in Peoria's hot climate, lasting 15-20 and 12-18 years respectively. They resist fading and staining better than standard plaster in Arizona's extreme conditions.

When should I resurface my Peoria pool?

Consider resurfacing if you notice rough texture, staining, cracks, or plaster loss. Most Peoria pools need resurfacing every 7-15 years depending on the finish type and maintenance. Free assessments available.

Can I upgrade from plaster to Pebble Tec in Peoria?

Absolutely. Upgrading from plaster to Pebble Tec is one of our most popular requests in Peoria. We remove the old plaster, prep the shell, and apply your chosen Pebble Tec finish. The result is a dramatic transformation.
