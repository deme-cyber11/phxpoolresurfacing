Phoenix residents understand their pools are more than just amenities; they're essential refuges from the intense Arizona sun and central to the Valley’s outdoor lifestyle. Hard water in Phoenix further contributes to mineral deposits and etching, making surfaces rough and prone to algae. Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing And Replastering in Phoenix

How much does pool resurfacing and replastering cost in Phoenix?

Costs for pool resurfacing in Phoenix vary significantly based on pool size, the chosen finish (e.g., standard plaster vs. quartz or pebble aggregates), and any necessary repairs to the shell or tile work. Factors like current material costs specific to the Arizona market and labor availability also play a role. We provide detailed, transparent quotes tailored to your specific Phoenix property and desired finish.

How long does pool resurfacing and replastering take for a Phoenix home?

For a typical residential pool commonly found in Phoenix neighborhoods, the entire process generally takes about 5 to 7 days of active work. This includes draining, surface preparation, plaster application, and initial curing. Larger pools or those requiring extensive tile and coping replacement, often seen in custom Phoenix homes, might extend the project timeline to 7-10 days.

How does Phoenix's hard water and intense sun affect my pool plaster?

Phoenix's exceptionally hard water, combined with relentless UV exposure, significantly accelerates plaster degradation. Minerals in the water can deposit and etch the surface, leading to roughness and staining, while constant sun breaks down the plaster's binding agents. This results in premature chalking, cracking, and a shorter lifespan for your pool's surface compared to cooler climates, making durable materials crucial.
