Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing in Scottsdale

What pool finishes are popular in Scottsdale?

Scottsdale homeowners prefer premium finishes like Pebble Tec, PebbleSheen, and quartz aggregate. These complement Scottsdale's luxury homes and last 15-20 years in the Arizona climate.

How much does Pebble Tec cost in Scottsdale?

Pebble Tec installation in Scottsdale ranges from $6,000-$12,000 depending on pool size and color selection. PebbleSheen, a smoother option, runs slightly higher. Free estimates available.

Can you resurface saltwater pools in Scottsdale?

Yes, we regularly resurface saltwater pools throughout Scottsdale. We use salt-compatible finishes and ensure proper bonding. Salt systems can be harder on surfaces, so choosing the right finish is important.

How do I maintain my pool finish in Scottsdale's heat?

Scottsdale's extreme heat and hard water require diligent chemical balancing. Keep pH between 7.2-7.6, brush weekly, and maintain proper calcium levels. We provide detailed aftercare instructions with every project.

Do you serve North Scottsdale and the surrounding areas?

Yes, we serve all of Scottsdale including North Scottsdale, South Scottsdale, McCormick Ranch, Gainey Ranch, DC Ranch, and surrounding communities. Free on-site consultations available.
