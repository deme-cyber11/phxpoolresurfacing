How long does pool resurfacing and replastering take for a Sun City home?

For most Sun City homes with average-sized pools (common in properties built in the 60s and 70s), the resurfacing and replastering process usually takes about 5 to 7 days, weather permitting. This includes draining, surface preparation, application of the new finish, and initial water refilling. Older pools sometimes require extra time for pre-existing crack repairs, but we strive for efficiency to minimize disruption.

How does Sun City's notorious hard water affect pool plaster, and what can PHX Pool Resurfacing do?

Sun City's hard water, rich in calcium and magnesium, can lead to scaling, etching, and a chalky residue on pool plaster over time, diminishing its appearance and feel. PHX Pool Resurfacing combats this by offering plaster additives designed to reduce mineral adhesion and recommending superior, acid-resistant finishes like pebble tech. These options provide a much more durable and visually appealing surface that stands up to Sun City’s unique water chemistry for years.
