Frequently Asked Questions — Pool Resurfacing in Sun City

How long does pool resurfacing take in Sun City?

Most Sun City pool resurfacing projects complete in 5 to 8 days for standard replasters. Older pools that need additional shell repairs or tile work may take 8 to 12 days. We work efficiently and communicate clearly throughout so Sun City homeowners know exactly what to expect.

What finish is best for a Sun City pool that has been replastered multiple times?

For pools that have had multiple replasters, we typically recommend removing all old plaster back to the gunite shell and applying a fresh quartz aggregate finish. This gives you a clean slate with a more durable surface that will last 15 to 20 years with proper care.

Do you work with Sun City HOA requirements?

Yes. Sun City has established community standards, and we are familiar with the requirements for pool work in this community. We handle all cleanup and ensure our work meets community expectations.
