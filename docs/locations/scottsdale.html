Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — Our certified technicians evaluate your pool's current surface condition, check for structural issues, and recommend the ideal finish for Scottsdale's desert climate and your home's aesthetic.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — We carefully drain the pool, remove loose or deteriorated material, and prep the shell with bond coating to ensure maximum adhesion for the new surface.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Our skilled crews hand-apply your chosen finish — whether white plaster, Pebble Tec, PebbleSheen, or quartz aggregate — using proven techniques refined over thousands of Scottsdale pools.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — The pool is filled on a controlled schedule and water chemistry is carefully balanced during the critical curing period to protect your new surface from staining or etching.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — We perform a comprehensive quality inspection, verify water chemistry, and walk you through aftercare instructions so your new finish lasts for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pool resurfacing cost in Scottsdale?

Pool resurfacing in Scottsdale typically ranges from $5,500 to $12,000 depending on pool size and finish type. Luxury communities like DC Ranch and Grayhawk often opt for premium Pebble Tec or quartz aggregate finishes, which fall in the $8,000-$12,000 range. Standard plaster resurfacing starts around $5,500. We provide free on-site estimates for every Scottsdale property.

What is the best pool finish for Scottsdale's climate?

Scottsdale's intense desert sun and hard water make durable aggregate finishes the top choice. Pebble Tec and quartz finishes resist UV damage, calcium scaling, and chemical wear far better than standard plaster. These premium surfaces can last 15-20 years in Scottsdale's climate compared to 7-10 years for basic plaster, making them a smart long-term investment for Scottsdale homeowners.

How long does pool resurfacing take in Scottsdale?

Most Scottsdale pool resurfacing projects take 5-7 business days from draining to the final water fill. The curing process requires an additional 7-10 days before swimming. We schedule around Scottsdale's peak summer heat when possible, as moderate temperatures between October and April provide optimal curing conditions for plaster and aggregate finishes.
