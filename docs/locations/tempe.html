Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — We thoroughly evaluate your pool's surface condition, check for cracks or delamination, and recommend the best finish option based on your budget and Tempe's hard water conditions.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — The pool is safely drained, old surface material is removed or chipped away, and the shell is prepped with a bonding agent to ensure the new finish adheres properly.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Our experienced crew hand-applies your selected finish — plaster, Pebble Tec, quartz, or PebbleSheen — with precision and care to create a smooth, durable surface.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — We begin filling the pool on a controlled schedule and monitor water chemistry throughout the curing period to prevent staining, etching, or discoloration on the fresh surface.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — A complete quality check is performed, water chemistry is verified, and we provide you with detailed maintenance instructions to maximize the lifespan of your new pool finish.
