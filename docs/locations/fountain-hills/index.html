Frequently Asked Questions — Pool Resurfacing in Fountain Hills

What premium pool finishes are popular in Fountain Hills?

Fountain Hills homeowners predominantly choose quartz aggregate or pebble finishes — typically PebbleTec or PebbleSheen — that complement the community's desert aesthetic and high home values. We offer the full range of premium finishes in colors that pair beautifully with Sonoran Desert landscaping.

How do I know when my Fountain Hills pool needs resurfacing?

Signs include rough texture that scratches feet, persistent staining that doesn't respond to chemical treatment, visible plaster chipping or delamination, and calcium nodules forming on the surface. Most Fountain Hills pools need resurfacing every 10 to 15 years depending on water chemistry management and original finish quality.
