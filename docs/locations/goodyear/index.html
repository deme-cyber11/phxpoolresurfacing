How does Goodyear's hard water impact pool surfaces over time?

Goodyear, like much of Arizona, experiences very hard water, which contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, this hard water can lead to significant issues for pool surfaces, particularly plaster. It causes unsightly scaling, mineral deposits, and etching, making the surface rough and porous. This not only detracts from your pool's appearance but can also foster algae growth and lead to premature deterioration of the plaster or aggregate finish if not regularly treated or, eventually, properly resurfaced.
