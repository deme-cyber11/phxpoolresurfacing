Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing And Replastering in Chandler

How much does pool resurfacing and replastering cost in Chandler?

The cost of pool resurfacing and replastering in Chandler varies significantly based on factors like pool size, chosen finish (e.g., basic white plaster vs. premium pebble finishes popular in areas like Ocotillo), and any necessary repairs. Due to high demand and specific material requirements for Chandler's climate, prices can range widely, but we provide transparent, competitive quotes tailored to your specific project and the local market conditions.

How long does pool resurfacing and replastering take for a Chandler home?

Typically, pool resurfacing and replastering for a standard Chandler residential pool takes approximately 1 to 2 weeks from draining to refilling. This timeline accounts for surface preparation, material application, curing time, and minor repairs often needed for pools exposed to our intense desert sun. Our efficient process minimizes disruption, allowing you to enjoy your renovated pool swiftly.

How does Chandler's hard water affect my pool plaster and what can be done?

Chandler's notoriously hard water is rich in minerals, which can aggressively react with pool plaster, leading to unsightly scaling, efflorescence, and a rough texture over time. This mineral buildup can also cause rapid deterioration. PHX Pool Resurfacing addresses this by recommending durable, high-performance finishes less susceptible to hard water etching, along with advising on effective water chemistry management and preventative treatments to protect your new surface.
