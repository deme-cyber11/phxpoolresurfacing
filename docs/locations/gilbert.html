Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — We conduct a detailed evaluation of your pool's surface, shell integrity, tile line, and equipment to determine the scope of work and recommend the best finish for your Gilbert home.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — The pool is drained safely, all loose or damaged material is removed, cracks and voids are repaired, and a professional bond coat is applied to prepare the shell for the new finish.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Your chosen finish — white plaster, Pebble Tec, quartz aggregate, or PebbleSheen — is expertly hand-applied by our trained crew to ensure a uniform, beautiful, and durable result.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — We fill the pool on a precise schedule and actively manage water chemistry during the curing process to prevent discoloration, etching, or other issues that can compromise the new surface.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — We perform a comprehensive quality review, confirm water balance, and provide you with a detailed care guide so your new pool finish delivers maximum longevity.
