Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — We assess your pool's surface, structure, tile line, and equipment to develop a comprehensive resurfacing plan tailored to your Chandler property and any applicable HOA guidelines.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — The pool is carefully drained, deteriorated material is removed, structural repairs are completed, and the shell receives a professional bond coat application for maximum adhesion.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Our crew hand-applies your selected finish — plaster, Pebble Tec, quartz aggregate, or PebbleSheen — ensuring even coverage and a beautiful, lasting result that complements your home.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — We manage the fill process on a controlled timeline, carefully balancing water chemistry during the critical curing phase to prevent staining and ensure proper surface hardening.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — A detailed quality inspection confirms the finish meets our high standards, water chemistry is verified, and we provide complete aftercare instructions for long-term surface protection.

What pool finishes are most popular in Chandler?

Chandler homeowners overwhelmingly prefer aggregate finishes like Pebble Tec and quartz because of their superior durability in Arizona's extreme heat and hard water. These finishes resist staining, scaling, and UV degradation far better than standard plaster, lasting 15-20 years compared to 7-10 for basic plaster. In communities like Ocotillo and Sun Groves, color-matched aggregate finishes are the most requested option.
