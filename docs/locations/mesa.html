Our Pool Resurfacing Process

  1. Pool Inspection & Assessment — Our team inspects your pool's shell, surface condition, tile line, and plumbing penetrations to identify all issues and recommend the right resurfacing approach for your Mesa property.
  2. Surface Preparation & Draining — We drain the pool safely, chip away deteriorated plaster or aggregate, repair any structural cracks, and apply bond coat to ensure optimal adhesion for the new finish.
  3. Plaster/Pebble Application — Your chosen finish — standard plaster, Pebble Tec, quartz aggregate, or PebbleSheen — is hand-applied by our experienced crew for a smooth, uniform, long-lasting result.
  4. Curing & Water Fill — The pool is filled on a carefully timed schedule while we monitor and balance water chemistry throughout the curing process to protect the integrity of the new surface.
  5. Final Testing & Walkthrough — We conduct a thorough quality inspection, test water balance, and walk you through proper maintenance guidelines to help your new finish last as long as possible.

How do I know if my Mesa pool needs resurfacing?

Common signs include rough texture that snags swimsuits or scrapes skin, visible staining that will not brush away, plaster flaking or peeling off the shell, persistent algae growth despite proper chemical balance, and visible cracks or hollow spots in the surface.
