Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Resurfacing And Replastering in Mesa

How much does pool resurfacing and replastering cost in Mesa?

Costs for pool resurfacing in Mesa fluctuate significantly based on factors like pool size, the chosen finish (from basic plaster to premium pebble or Hydrazzo), and any necessary repairs. Given Mesa's varied housing stock, older pools might require more extensive preparation, impacting the final price. Typically, prices range from $4,500 to $15,000+, reflecting material quality and labor specific to the competitive Phoenix metro market conditions.

How long does pool resurfacing and replastering take for a Mesa home?

For an average-sized Mesa home, a pool resurfacing project generally takes 5-7 days from the initial draining to refilling, excluding the plaster curing period. Larger or older pools, common in areas like Mesa's central corridor or near Fiesta Lakes, might extend the timeline slightly due to more involved preparation. Smaller, newer pools in communities such as Eastmark can sometimes be completed more quickly.

How does Mesa's hard water affect new pool plaster?

Mesa's naturally hard water, rich in mineral content, can lead to calcium buildup and scale deposits on new plaster if not managed correctly. We strongly recommend specific post-resurfacing water balancing protocols and professional startup services to mitigate these effects. This proactive approach helps preserve your new surface, maintaining its pristine appearance longer against the challenges posed by local water conditions.
