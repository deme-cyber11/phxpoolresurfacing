What Is Terms?

At PHX Pool Resurfacing, "Terms" refers to our Tailored Erosion-Resistant Modern Surfacing – a premium, advanced resurfacing and replastering system specifically engineered for superior performance and aesthetics. Unlike conventional plastering, Terms utilizes a proprietary blend of high-grade aggregates, modified cementitious materials, and specialized polymers. This sophisticated composition is meticulously mixed and expertly applied to create an exceptionally smooth, robust, and highly resistant finish. The process involves a deep understanding of material science to ensure optimal bond strength, reduced porosity, and enhanced resistance to the harsh elements, chemical imbalances, and general wear and tear that Phoenix pools frequently experience. Terms provides a long-lasting, vibrant surface that not only looks stunning but also offers unparalleled durability and ease of maintenance.

Common Questions About Terms

How much does Terms cost in Phoenix, AZ?

The cost of Terms resurfacing in Phoenix, AZ, varies significantly based on pool size, chosen finish materials (e.g., quartz vs. pebble aggregates), and the extent of prep work or repairs needed. How long does Terms take?

The typical timeframe for a professional Terms resurfacing project, from draining the pool to refilling it, usually ranges from 5 to 10 business days. How do I know if I need Terms?

Several signs indicate your pool may benefit from our Terms service. These include visible cracks or chips in the plaster, a rough or abrasive surface that irritates skin, stubborn staining or discoloration that won't clean, frequent algae growth, or noticeable plaster delamination. If your pool is losing water due to surface porosity or simply looks outdated and dull, it's likely time to consider a Terms upgrade.
